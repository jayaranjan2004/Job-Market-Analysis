# Job Market Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes job market data to identify employment trends, salary patterns, in-demand skills, hiring companies, and geographical distribution of job opportunities.

The objective is to generate actionable insights that can help job seekers understand the data science job market and support data-driven decision-making.

## Dataset
- **Source:** DataMites PRDA-04
- **Dataset:** Market.csv
- **Records:** 742 job postings
- **Features:** 42 columns

The dataset contains information about job titles, salary estimates, companies, locations, industries, sectors, required skills, education, and other job-related attributes.

## Project Objectives
- Analyze job postings by state.
- Compare minimum, maximum, and average salaries across states.
- Identify the top 5 industries with the highest number of data science job postings.
- Identify companies with the most job openings.
- Analyze the most common job titles.
- Compare salaries across popular job titles.
- Identify skills required for different job roles.
- Analyze the relationship between salary and education.
- Develop visualizations and derive actionable insights.
